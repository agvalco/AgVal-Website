What We Appraise

Specialized expertise across Indiana's land and commercial market

From a 600-acre row crop operation to a downtown commercial building, we bring the right methodology, comparable data, and local knowledge to every assignment.

Agricultural Valuation

Productive farmland, row crop, pasture, dairy and livestock operations, and specialty agriculture. Backed by soil productivity ratings, yield data, and Indiana market comparables.

Maximize value and negotiate from a position of evidence.

Rural Residential

Country homes, hobby farms, equestrian estates, and acreage properties where standard residential models fall short. We value the land, improvements, and lifestyle accurately.

Reports lenders accept and buyers and sellers trust.

Rural & Recreational

Timberland, hunting tracts, waterfront, recreational acreage, and conservation property. We understand what drives value beyond just buildable acres.

Capture the full premium of recreational and timber assets.

General Commercial

Retail, office, industrial, mixed-use, and special purpose property. Income, sales comparison, and cost approach analysis tailored to the assignment.

Decision-grade valuations for financing, tax, and litigation.
